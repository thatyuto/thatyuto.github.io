Shot Replay: finding shots in football match video

Give it a half of football video and it returns a timeline of shots; click a time and the video jumps to two seconds before the shot. Trained on SoccerNet broadcast footage with a fine-tuned ResNet18 over 4-second, 8-frame windows, then evaluated at the event level (precision and recall within a 2-second tolerance) rather than by window accuracy. The final step tests it on my own sideline footage to measure the gap between broadcast and fixed-camera video.
