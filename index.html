Shot Replay

Give it a half of football video and it returns a timeline of shots; click a time and the video jumps to two seconds before the shot. A fine-tuned ResNet18 over 4-second, 8-frame windows, trained on SoccerNet and evaluated at the event level (precision and recall within 2 seconds). The last step tests it on my own sideline footage.

2026 · PyTorch · ResNet18 · SoccerNet · Event detection
